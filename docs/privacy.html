SleepWindow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28

SleepWindow is a sleep-timing planner. Your sleep entries, wake times, saved presets, and settings live on your device and never leave it.

What lives on your device only

All of your settings, saved presets, wake and bedtime entries, and reminder schedules are stored locally on your device using UserDefaults, a standard iOS mechanism. This data is never transmitted off your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What we collect (anonymous product-interaction analytics)

SleepWindow uses PostHog Cloud (us.i.posthog.com) to understand which features are useful and which are not. Events are anonymous and tied only to a random per-install identifier that we generate locally. We collect:

  • Product Interaction — which screens you view, which features you tap, when the paywall is shown, whether you subscribe.
  • Crash Data — a low-cardinality, PII-free crash signature via Apple's MetricKit if the app crashes.
  • Performance Data — session length, screens per session, days since install.
  • Other Diagnostic Data — error codes and message keys for user-visible failure paths (e.g. StoreKit product-load failures).

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, Apple ID, physical address, advertising ID, or your wake times, bedtime entries, saved presets, or reminder times. We do not use PostHog session replay, person profiles, or lifecycle-event autocapture. SleepWindow does not request the App Tracking Transparency permission and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Search Ads attribution (Apple)

On first launch SleepWindow makes one call to Apple's Search Ads attribution service (api-adservices.apple.com) using AAAttribution. Apple tells us only whether you arrived from an Apple Search Ad and, if so, the campaign / adGroup / org identifiers — no personal information. That value is attached to a single app.first_open event so we can tell paid installs from organic. You can opt out of Apple's ad attribution in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

Notifications

If you enable a bedtime reminder, SleepWindow schedules a local notification using iOS's notification system. Nothing about the reminder is sent to us or anyone else. You can disable reminders at any time in the app's Settings or in the iOS Settings app.

Payments

All in-app purchases (Monthly, Yearly, and Lifetime) are processed by Apple. We do not see your payment details. Apple provides us with an anonymous transaction identifier for entitlement verification only.

Opting out of analytics

You can turn off anonymous analytics at any time in the app: Settings → About → "Share anonymous usage data". After you turn it off, no further events are sent from your device.

Children

SleepWindow is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone under 13.
